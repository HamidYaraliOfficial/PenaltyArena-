# Changelog
## v1.0.0 — 2026
- First release: 3D penalty shootout (Three.js + Electron), Windows x64.
- Modes: full match with sudden death, training, goalkeeper challenge.
- Ball physics (gravity, drag, Magnus), post/bar collisions, net, AI goalkeeper and kicker, 5 difficulty levels.
- Stadium with crowd, ad boards, floodlights, dugouts, tunnel; night / dusk / day.
- Detailed player models with kits, numbers and animations. Procedural audio. Persian UI. Installer, uninstaller, portable build.
## v1.0.1
- New human models: shaped torso, tapered limbs, knees and elbows, painted face, hair, striped kit, collar, numbered shirt, boots, goalkeeper gloves.
- Kicker now visible on screen during the run-up; crowd members have heads and skin tones.
